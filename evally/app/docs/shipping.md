# Shipping

Updated: October 2026

| Option | Delivery time | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 3-5 business days | $4.99, free on orders over $35 |
| Express | 1-2 business days | $12.99 |

Orders placed before 2 pm Eastern on a business day ship the same day. We ship to all 50 US states. Each order gets a tracking link by email when it ships.

Books that are out of stock ship when they come back in; we email you a date.
