# Returns and refunds

Updated: September 2026

You can return any book within **30 days of delivery**. Books must be unread and in the condition you received them. Gift cards and e-books can't be returned.

To start a return, give us your order number and the email on the order. We'll email a prepaid return label.

Once the book reaches our warehouse, we refund the original payment method within **5-7 business days**. Original shipping costs aren't refunded unless the book arrived damaged or we sent the wrong item.

Returns after 30 days are not accepted.
