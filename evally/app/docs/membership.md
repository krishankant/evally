# Bramble Club membership

Updated: August 2026

Bramble Club costs **$29 a year**. Members get:

- 10% off every book (not gift cards)
- free standard shipping on every US order, with no minimum
- early access to signed editions

You can cancel any time from your account page. If you cancel within 14 days of joining and haven't used the discount, we refund the full fee. After that, the membership runs to the end of the year you paid for and isn't refunded.
