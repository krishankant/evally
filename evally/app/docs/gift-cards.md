# Gift cards

Updated: June 2026

Bramble Books gift cards come in any amount from $10 to $200 and **never expire**. They can be used for books and memberships.

Gift cards **can't be exchanged for cash or refunded**, except where the law requires it. A lost card can be replaced if you have the purchase receipt or the card number.

If you pay with a gift card and return the book, the refund goes back onto a gift card.
