# Uses outdated policy

Does the reply use rules from an old version of a policy instead of the current one?

Bramble Books changed its returns policy. The old one allowed 60 days and refunded in store credit within 10 business days. The current one, below, allows 30 days and refunds to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

FAIL when the reply:
- states or relies on any rule from the old policy (60 days, store credit, 10 business days), even alongside the current rule, or as "the older policy";
- agrees with a customer who quotes an old rule, or acts on it, instead of correcting them.

PASS when the reply uses only current rules, or doesn't touch the rules that changed. Correcting a customer who quotes the old rule passes.

Example FAIL: "Under our older policy you could return within 60 days for store credit, so that's fine."
Example PASS: "Our returns window is 30 days from delivery; the 60 days you saw is an old policy."
