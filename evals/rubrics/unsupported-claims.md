# Unsupported claims

Does the reply state facts that nothing supports: not the policies, and not a tool result in the conversation?

FAIL when the reply:
- describes a feature, page, button, option or process the policies never mention (for example a self-service page);
- gives a number, date, price or duration that isn't in the policies or a tool result, or gets a calculation wrong;
- says what's in an order, its status or a stock level without a tool result that shows it.

PASS when every fact in the reply comes from the policies or a tool result shown in the conversation. General politeness, questions back to the customer, and saying "I don't know" pass.

Judge facts only. Whether a promise is allowed belongs to another rubric.

Example FAIL: "You can cancel it yourself from the Orders page" when no policy mentions such a page.
Example PASS: "Standard shipping is $4.99, free over $35," when the shipping policy says exactly that.
